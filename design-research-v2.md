# Light redesign: 12 October 2026

The supplied Velour screenshot is the primary art-direction reference. This is an original composition, with no borrowed vendor photography, campaign wording or proprietary components.

## Reference observations

**Apple Mac: https://www.apple.com/mac/**

Inspected live desktop navigation, family overview and desktop gallery, including changing the category control. Useful patterns: isolated product visuals, consistent family grammar, white/soft-grey rhythm, rounded gallery media and pill selection. Applied: curated systems, comparisons in a focused dialog and fewer specifications on the homepage.

**Vercel: https://vercel.com/home**

Inspected the live homepage and Products disclosure. Sparse typography and navigation hierarchy are useful; the distinctive triangle, branded illustrations, logos and claims are excluded. Applied: disciplined type, restrained navigation and short decisions. The lighter direction supersedes the original dense bordered-grid influence.

**Watermelon UI: https://ui.watermelon.sh/components/tabs and /components/button**

Reviewed live gallery and public documentation. Compared inset pill controls, raised active pills, outline states and tab layouts. Applied: original soft-shadow audience control, rounded selections and consistent button states. No full template was copied.

**The Component Gallery: https://component.gallery/components/segmented-control/ and /components/tabs/**

Compared controls across design systems. Applied: semantics based on purpose, keyboard navigation, focus visibility and mobile presentation suited to the content. Radix primitives provide the production interaction layer.

**Variant: https://variant.com/**

Inspected the public interface and embedded widget gallery without signing in or submitting a generation prompt. Physical radio and battery widgets show how material depth makes controls tangible. Applied selectively to the audience control and small explanatory visuals. The dark application frame is excluded.

**React Spring: https://www.react-spring.dev/docs/components/use-transition**

Reviewed transition lifecycle, exit-before-enter and reduced-motion utility. Applied: short state transitions and a damped sliding audience selector, without continuous movement or scroll hijacking.

## Design decisions

- White canvas, pale neutral surfaces, graphite type and a small cool-blue accent.
- Original silver cutouts provide the main sculptural product moment.
- A broad rounded frame and asymmetry connect to Velour's editorial composition.
- Mobile has a readable headline above the product, rather than a scaled desktop split composition.
- Soft shadows are confined to controls and small visual objects; glass treatment to navigation and dialog backdrops.
- Remove chapter labels, crosshairs, edition numbers, mono annotations and repeated qualifications from the homepage.
- Keep necessary configuration and preview limitations in catalogue, details, review and legal pages.
- Open Middle East positioning, without explicit country positioning.
